# Managed partnership workflow

All In Streams connects businesses with creators and agencies. Staff coordinate matching, negotiation, selection, separate agreements, delivery review and manual settlement.

## Accounts

- **Business:** creates and edits its own campaign briefs, reviews proposals and requests a preferred provider through All In Streams.
- **Streamer:** an individual creator with at least one Twitch or Kick channel, audience countries and a private short profile/audience verification video.
- **Influencer:** an individual creator with at least one Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok profile, audience countries and a private short verification video. Streamers and influencers have separate staff directories and agency roster categories.
- **Agency:** has a separate agency profile and talent roster. It can request creators for its clients, supply talent, or do both. A provider invitation belongs to the agency account; the agency can propose talent from its own approved roster.
- **Admin:** reviews profiles and briefs, invites providers, negotiates terms, chooses the final provider, manages private documents and validates delivery and settlement records.

Agency client briefs and agency provider opportunities are separate. Acting as a provider does not grant access to another business's brief or commercial terms.

Every new account must provide a valid Telegram contact. Individual creator registration cannot finish without the correct type of profile link, audience countries and a playable verification video. The system checks file type, size and duration; staff review the actual profile and analytics shown in the recording. All contacts, social links and verification videos remain private to staff. Existing users can update their Telegram at Account settings.

Staff control video upload size, duration and optional compression in Video uploads. They may watch or delete a proof video; after deletion, a new video is required before profile resubmission. Staff can also attach several private PDF/DOC/DOCX files to any account. These internal account files are separate from party-specific campaign agreements and cannot be read by the account holder.

## Brief and matching

The brief owner supplies brand and campaign details, vertical, audience countries, language, platform, a streamer/influencer/agency preference, deliverables, dates, total budget, currency, commercial model and campaign objectives. Staff review the brief and either request changes, decline it or approve matching. Approval for matching does not launch a campaign. Staff can filter each creator directory by residence, audience country, platform and maximum EUR rate.

During matching, staff may send an opportunity to several approved creators or approved supplying agencies. Each invitation has its own provider compensation and offer version. Staff prepare a neutral opportunity description and provider requirements; the full business brief is private.

An invitation contains the opportunity code, campaign category, audience countries, language, platform, schedule, provider deliverables, provider fee, optional CPA rate and provider requirements. Providers do not receive the company name, business contact details, raw campaign title, full business brief, total business budget, All In Streams margin, other providers' offers or internal staff notes.

## Replies, negotiation and final selection

Providers can express interest, decline or propose changes. A change request includes a note and may include a requested fixed fee or CPA rate. Agency replies may also propose talent from their approved roster. Replies and revised offers retain a negotiation history.

Staff can revise the provider's offer. A revised offer requires a fresh provider response; an earlier acceptance does not accept a new offer version. Expressing interest does not select a provider or launch a campaign.

The business may review an anonymized shortlist and request a preferred streamer, influencer or agency. Only staff make the final selection. Exactly one accepted, approved creator or agency is selected for a brief; the remaining invitations are closed as not selected. Provider and company identities, profile links and contacts always remain private to administrators. Historical introduction timestamps never grant access to an identity. All communication and separate agreements go through All In Streams.

## Agreements and launch

The business signs its agreement with All In Streams. The selected creator or agency signs a separate provider agreement with All In Streams. Documents are private to their specified party or internal staff. Uploading a file does not mark it signed; staff must verify the signed agreement.

Provider document selectors accept only the selected provider who accepted the current offer for that campaign. A business cannot download provider commercial terms, and a provider cannot download the business's agreement.

Launch requires the selected provider, current acceptance, verified separate signed agreements and payment readiness. Payment readiness is an administrative prerequisite; it does not process a payment.

## Delivery and reporting

During a live campaign, the selected provider submits a content URL, delivery date, notes and optional private PDF or image evidence, up to 10 MB. It may also submit self-reported impressions, clicks and conversions. Providers cannot set their own review status.

Staff approve evidence or request changes with a provider-visible note. Returned evidence can be corrected and resubmitted while the campaign is live. Approved evidence is locked until staff reopen it; completed campaigns cannot reopen evidence through this workflow. Completion requires approved delivery evidence for the selected provider.

Self-reported provider counts do not automatically become campaign performance statistics or validated CPA conversions. Staff maintain campaign reporting separately after checking the results. Evidence files are available only to the owning provider and staff.

## Manual payment ledger

Staff record payment milestones for the selected provider after contracts begin. Milestones identify fixed fees, validated CPA or another agreed amount in the accepted offer's currency. Provider accounts see only their own milestones.

A milestone moves from **pending** to **approved**, then to **paid** when staff record an actual external payment date and optional provider reference. Approval is required before recording payment. The portal does not connect to a payment processor or transfer money. Estimated or self-reported conversions do not create automatic payouts.

## Creator removal

Staff can remove a creator from future matching while retaining historical invitations, agreements and reporting. A creator involved in selected active work must be resolved before removal. Archived records preserve the campaign history; removing a creator does not erase commercial records.

## Reference precedence

The supplied journey screenshots and videos establish the managed matching, invitations, protected shortlist, separate contracts, evidence review and manual settlement stages. The latest requirement is stricter than the older creator video: company and provider identities and contacts are never released to counterparties. Final selection is one individual streamer/influencer or one agency, even when several providers were invited.
